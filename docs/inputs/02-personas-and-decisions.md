# EXAMPLE — Personas and decisions

## Persona 1 — Portfolio Director (primary)
- **Function:** Operations / PMO
- **Decisions in this concept:** Which projects need executive attention this week; whether to reallocate senior resources; whether a project is "red" for the capital review pack
- **Frequency:** Daily scan; deep dive 2–3×/week
- **Looks at first:** Portfolio heat map / ranked risk list
- **Escalates to:** VP Operations / sponsor
- **Abandon signal:** If they cannot tell *why* a project is red in two clicks

## Persona 2 — Project Manager (primary walker of deep journeys)
- **Function:** Project delivery
- **Decisions:** What to fix this week; which issues to escalate; whether cost vs schedule tradeoff is acceptable
- **Frequency:** Daily
- **Looks at first:** Their project's health summary and open critical issues
- **Escalates to:** Portfolio Director; functional leads
- **Abandon signal:** If drill-down does not match how they talk about drivers (cost, schedule, safety, change)

## Persona 3 — Cost Lead (secondary reviewer)
- **Decisions:** Whether cost variance story is credible; whether contingency draw needs approval
- **Frequency:** Weekly + ad hoc
- **Abandon signal:** Fake-looking cost rollups or missing contingency context

## Persona 4 — Safety Lead (secondary)
- **Decisions:** Whether safety trend warrants stop-work conversation
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Abandon signal:** Safety buried or not actionable
