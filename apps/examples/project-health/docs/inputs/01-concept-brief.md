# EXAMPLE — Concept brief: Project Health Command Hub

## One-sentence concept
A single decision-oriented hub where portfolio and project leaders see construction project health, drill into drivers, and decide what to escalate—without opening five source systems.

## Problem
Today, project health signals are fragmented across scheduling, cost, safety, RFIs/change orders, and field reporting tools. PMs stitch weekly decks manually. Functional leads (cost, schedule, safety) each trust "their" system. Leadership decisions lag, and early-warning signals are missed until a steering meeting.

## Why now
- Portfolio pressure on margin and schedule variance across active jobs
- Leadership asked for a "command" view before the next quarterly capital review
- Appetite to validate UX and decision pathways with a clickable prototype before funding a full integration program

## Decision this prototype unlocks
If PMs and functional leads can complete priority journeys on a stubbed Angular hub and agree the decision moments are right, we green-light a Phase 1 build (Angular shell + first two data domains) vs. kill or re-scope.

## Non-goals (prototype)
- Live connections to ERP, scheduling, or safety systems
- Auth/SSO production hardening
- Mobile-native apps
- Full portfolio analytics / BI replacement

## Adjacent initiatives
- Existing PMO weekly status ritual (must not contradict)
- Planned data warehouse work (prototype must not depend on it)
