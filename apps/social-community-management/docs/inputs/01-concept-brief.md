# Concept brief — Social Community Management

## Working title

Social Community Management

## Core decision

Community managers spot fan feature signals on social channels and route them so product can decide accept / defer / reject.

## Problem today

- Fan bugs and feature ideas feel ignored or slow to reach product.
- Fans want more say in the roadmap.
- Jira tickets from feedback often sit unassigned; fans do not see a closed loop.

## Non-goals

- Live X API, live Jira, live external feeds (all mocked).

## Feasibility map (docs only)

X (mock), Jira (mock), internal backend/observability (mock), Microsoft Teams (mock, deferred).
